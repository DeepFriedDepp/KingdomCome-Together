# What changed in WO-136 (for both players)

**Kingdom Come: Together.** Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by
Warhorse Studios.

This comes with the next installer (WO-140). The checks to do together are in
`docs/TWO-PLAYER-CHECKLIST.md`, section WO-136. In plain words:

- **Joining is calmer.** While a world loads, the mod no longer touches any
  NPC or the other player's figure. The loading screen should end normally,
  without the red "Loading screen timeouted" text.
- **The host's animals are on both screens.** Wolves, wild dogs and boars that
  appear in the host's world now appear for the partner too, where they are.
  Their bites still land (the host's world decides them), but the partner does
  not see the bite animation yet.
- **Whoever rides a horse owns it.** The partner can ride the host's horses:
  the horse no longer disappears under them. When you get off, it stays where
  you left it on both screens.
- **Knocked-out enemies go down for the partner too**, also in the middle of a
  fight.
- **Enemies fight both of you.** Hitting an enemy makes it turn to you; if the
  host goes down, the enemies keep fighting the partner instead of giving up.
- **Clothes match.** The other player's figure wears only their real clothes —
  no more guard armour stuck on it.
- **Torches show.** A lit torch in the other player's hand is shown, and put
  away when they put theirs away.
- **Crouching shows** both ways.
- **Looting:** "Someone already took that" now appears only when the other
  player really took the item first.

Still known: the partner does not see animals' bite animations; mounting the
host's horse may show "Mount and steal" — tell us what happens if you press it.
