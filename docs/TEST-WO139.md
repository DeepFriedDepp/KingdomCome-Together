# What changed in WO-139 (for both players)

**Kingdom Come: Together.** Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by
Warhorse Studios.

This comes with the next installer (WO-140). The checks to do together are in
`docs/TWO-PLAYER-CHECKLIST.md`, section WO-139. In plain words:

- **The partner's crimes count in the host's world.** Stealing, picking a
  lock, taking someone's horse, robbing a body, walking into someone's home:
  if people of the host's world see the partner do it, it is a crime there. If
  nobody sees it, it isn't — as in the game.
- **Guards deal with the partner.** A guard who saw it, or who was told by a
  witness, comes to the partner and arrests them. The arrest is the game's
  own, on the partner's screen: reply, surrender or refuse, then pay the fine,
  accept the punishment, talk your way out, or fight. Meanwhile the guard
  waits in the host's world. What is decided counts for both.
- **Violence is answered with force.** Hit or kill someone in front of a guard
  and the host's guards fight the partner. Once the partner is down, or comes
  back later, the next guard arrests them instead.
- **No time jump for punishment.** The stocks and jail no longer skip hours:
  the clock is shared, so it keeps running for both of you.
- **Execution** is a death for the partner: they wake outside the town, near
  the host, and the crime is gone.
- **Nothing between the two of you is a crime.** Hitting each other by
  accident, or a death at the other's hand, raises nothing.
- **You can't rob each other.** Looting or pickpocketing the other player is
  refused: "You can't steal from each other in co-op."
- **The host's horses are free to ride** for the partner (no "Mount and
  steal"). A townsperson's horse is still a theft.
- **Off switch:** the host can type `mp_crime_shared off` in the console (`~`)
  to stop sharing crimes, and `mp_crime_shared on` to start again.

Still known: the partner's crimes don't change the host's reputation (the
partner's own reputation changes on their own side); a guard the partner
resisted may attack them on sight for a few minutes, as in the game; the
host's guards never search the partner for stolen goods.
